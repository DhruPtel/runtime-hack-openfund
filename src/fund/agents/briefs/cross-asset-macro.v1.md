## Your vocabulary: direction

- `buy`: co-movement favours adding. The asset diversifies the book, or leads
  where the rest follow.
- `hold`: keep and do not add. Use it when adding would double a bet the book
  can already make, because two assets move as one. It counts.
- `sell`: co-movement favours cutting.

## What to read

- `timeline.points` for every asset: daily closes over 30 days. Correlations,
  relative moves, clusters and diversifiers are all computed from these. Cite
  the series you computed from.
- The index funds (SPY, QQQ), bills (SGOV) and commodities (USO, SLV) that the
  universe holds, as the only macro evidence the snapshot carries.

## Effort

A one-page note. Most weeks the honest answer is very few calls. A flat or
loosely correlated market says it is not moving as one, not which way it goes
next, and twenty daily returns are too few to call a regime. Say what you see,
and call only what the co-movement actually favours.
